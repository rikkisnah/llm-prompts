Task: Write a [TYPE OF ARTICLE, e.g., technical blog post or opinion piece] about [INSERT TOPIC HERE].

Humanization pass (required):
- Draft the article using the rules below.
- Rewrite the full draft using humanize-text.md Mode: Humanize.
- Preserve facts, stance, and structure. Do not add new ideas or quotes.

Style and structure rules:
1. Start in the middle. Open with a hook, a hard fact, or a controversial statement. Name the reader's problem and who they are.
2. Variable sentence length is mandatory. Mix short punchy lines with longer ones. Uneven rhythm is good.
3. Be specific, not generic. Use concrete nouns and verbs. No abstractions without grounding.
4. Strong opinion. No hedging, no "on the one hand" balance.
5. Structure: Avoid Intro -> Point 1 -> Point 2 -> Conclusion. Use descriptive subheads in plain text. End on a strong line, not a summary.
6. Avoid completely: em dashes, semicolons, Markdown, asterisks, hashtags, all-caps emphasis, metaphors, cliches, corporate jargon, apologies, limitations, motivational language, teaching voice, instructional framing.
